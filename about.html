Our point of view

Material-led is a standard, not a slogan.

We care about the finished feeling: calm, considered and ready to be used.

We believe a room should feel collected, not filled. Our studio pairs enduring materials with practical proportions, working closely with clients, makers, and installers from first sketch to final placement. That means taking the time to ask better questions, keeping recommendations grounded in real life, and treating every space, surface and belonging with respect.

We are small enough to notice the details and experienced enough to keep the bigger picture in view. The result is work that feels personal without becoming precious.
